過去の肩書き（本質）ではなく、今の選択と行動（実存）だけが私を定義する。常に領域を越境し、自らを再構築し続ける私の行動原理そのものです。

It is not past titles (essence), but present choices and actions (existence) that define me. This is the core principle behind my constant drive to transcend boundaries and continuously reinvent myself.
